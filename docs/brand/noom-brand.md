**Tags:** behavior-change weight loss programs, prescription GLP-1 medication access, telehealth coaching, online weight-loss counseling, AI-assisted habit tracking

## Positioning

### Value Proposition

Noom is a consumer-led digital health platform that helps people lose weight and keep it off by combining psychology-based behavior change with access to prescription weight-loss medications, clinician support, coaching, and AI tools in one online program. Its integrated “GLP-1 as a bridge” approach, including a GLP-1 Companion and tailored, safety-first clinical protocols, is designed to drive results beyond medication alone, including the claim that Noom Med users lose 48% more weight than medication alone.

### Target Audience

- **Adults with overweight or obesity seeking FDA-approved prescription weight-loss medications (including GLP-1s) plus ongoing lifestyle and habit coaching** — They need eligibility screening, clinician-led telehealth prescribing, medication shipped on a tailored dosing plan, and a psychology-based program to help them lose weight and keep it off.
- **Adults who want a psychology-based weight-loss program to change eating habits and sustain results, with or without medication** — They need an evidence-based behavior-change platform with coaching, tools for tracking and routines, and support that helps them manage the “why” behind habits and maintain long-term weight control.
- **Employers, health plans, and health systems looking to offer weight-management programs to members or employees** — They need a scalable digital health solution that combines behavior-change programming with optional access to medication-supported pathways and clinician/coaching support.

### Products & Services

- **Noom Weight** (product) — Psychology-based weight-loss program that pairs behavioral science with personalized coaching and app tools to help members lose weight and keep it off.
- **Noom Med** (service) — Clinician-led, online access to weight-loss medications (including GLP-1s) with medications shipped to your door when prescribed and ongoing medication plus lifestyle support.
- **Noom GLP-1 Rx Program** (service) — Dual-pathway GLP-1 weight-loss program with a personalized plan and Noom GLP-1 Companion support to help maximize results and sustain habits.
- **Noom GLP-1 Rx Plus** (service) — Premium GLP-1 Rx offering that expands Noom’s medication-plus-habits approach for members starting on higher-support clinical tracks.
- **Noom Microdose GLP-1 Rx** (service) — Low-dose GLP-1 medication program designed for a smaller starting dose with an online clinical pathway and continued behavior-change preparation.
- **Noom GLP-1 Companion with Muscle Defense** (product) — Companion app experience that includes protein tracking, high-protein recipes, and on-demand workouts to help preserve muscle while losing fat alongside GLP-1 use.

## Brand

### Voice

Noom writes in a confident, science-forward yet encouraging tone that emphasizes long-term behavior change alongside medication. The copy often frames GLP-1 as “a bridge” and repeatedly reassures people with practical, optimistic language like “Lose weight and keep it off” and “We believe behavior is essential to lasting change.”

**Attributes:** confident, measured, empowering, factual

**Avoid:** cutesy, hard sell, stuffy

### Style

Noom’s visual identity centers on a bold red Noom wordmark/logo and clean, modern sans-serif typography, paired with high-contrast black body text and minimal UI styling. Pages use large photographic hero imagery with big statement headlines, then switch to structured content cards and sections with muted pastel backgrounds (notably soft greens/blues), plus prominent dark, rounded primary buttons using white type.

**Attributes:** modern, clean, approachable, high-contrast
